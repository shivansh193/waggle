# Waggle — Product steering

## What it is
Waggle is a browser-based motion controller kit and two original party games, built for Kiroween 2026. It resurrects Wii-era motion controls using phone sensors — no app, no pairing, no account. Scan a QR code and your phone becomes a controller.

## The two original games
- **Séance** (2–8 players, co-op): all players tilt their phones; the planchette follows the average tilt. Shake together to banish a ghost.
- **Hex Duel** (1v1): draw a shape in the air (circle = shield, zigzag = bolt, spiral = curse), then flick to cast. Custom spells via teach-a-move.

## Naming rules
- Never say "Wii", "Nintendo", "Kinect", or any Nintendo product name in the product, UI, or code comments. These belong in the write-up's historical context only.
- Both games are original designs. No copying existing game mechanics, names, or look.

## Target experience
- A judge joins from their own phone in under 15 seconds, with no install.
- Both games are playable with 2+ players on iOS Safari and Android Chrome.
- The core technical claim: **motion controls you can unit-test** (record a trace, replay it in CI).
